# Example cover letter — THE canonical format to mimic

This is an EXAMPLE of a good cover letter. When Argus generates a new one
(`cover N`), it reads this file and imitates its structure, length (5 short
paragraphs, 180–260 words) and tone. Rules this example embodies, always to
respect:

- NO em dashes "—": normal sentences with commas or periods.
- Do NOT copy sentences from the offer: give a genuine reason anchored in real
  experience (a concrete project, tool or result from the CV, and what it
  taught you).
- The languages paragraph is ONLY languages. No loose adjective-list sentences.
- Be honest about gaps (a tool not yet mastered: said naturally, with eagerness
  to learn it).
- Formal salutation, default "Dear Hiring Manager," (not mandatory). Closing
  "Best regards,".

Replace the body below with your own best letter, or let the setup ask for one.

---

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the position of [Role] at [Company]. I am a [field]
engineer with hands-on experience in [area]. I am based in [City] and open to
the role's location, so joining your team would not involve any relocation
difficulties.

During my [internship/role] at [Company], I [did something concrete], for
example building [tool/pipeline] that [outcome]. Doing this well required a
clear, maintainable structure, since other members of the team relied on it.
That experience taught me to value [a genuine lesson] when a wider team shares
tools.

I hold a [Degree] from [Institution], where my coursework covered [topics],
along with hands-on work using [tools]. I would welcome the opportunity to apply
this foundation to [what the role does] and to contribute to the work your team
is developing.

I am fluent in [languages], and I am currently improving my [language], which is
at a [level] level.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to
discuss my profile further at interview.

Best regards,
Your Name
