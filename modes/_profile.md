## Never mention (in cover letters, CV or interviews)

- e.g. a qualification you removed from your CV — do not mention it at all.

## Cover-letter tone

- Formal, direct, concise. Length: ~5 short paragraphs, 180–260 words total.
- Language: match the offer's language; default to English.
- Never invent facts that are not in `cv.md`.
